In short

You are buying 3 managed reinforcement-learning cycles to move Held-out pass rate for Internal workflow automation from 60% to a target of 80%, using the model Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, over 6 weeks. The total is $210,000. You approve every cycle, and each invoice is issued when its milestone completes.

Words used in this proposal

RL cycle
One round of training the model on its own attempts, scoring the attempts, and updating the model.
Rollout
One attempt by the model at a task, recorded with its result.
Held-out evaluation
A test set the model never trains on, used to measure real improvement.
Grader
The rules that score an attempt, for example pass or fail, or a rubric score.
Compute allowance
The training compute included in the price, stated in GPU-hours for the whole engagement.
Checkpoint
A saved version of the trained model, with its exact settings, so results can be reproduced.

Common questions

Why do I pay for the foundation before training starts?

Training is only as good as the test that measures it. The foundation builds that test, the grader and a baseline, and you keep them. It is the one part of the work that makes every later cycle measurable.

Why is engineering separate from the cycles?

Engineers keep the loop running whether or not a given cycle improves the score. Their effort follows the number of cycles, so it is billed with them.

Why does the price depend on the model?

Larger models need more compute and more rollouts to learn the same task, so each cycle costs more to run. The price tier reflects that.

What if a cycle does not improve the score?

You decide after each cycle whether to continue, change direction or stop. You pay for the cycles and milestones completed. We do not stop contracted work silently, and we do not charge for work you have stopped before it starts.

What happens if I stop early?

You pay for approved work completed and for any non-cancellable third-party compute committed for your engagement, net of reasonable reuse. The handoff fee is billed only on a signed handoff.

Acceptance

Each phase is accepted against the objective evidence listed in the statement of work, and each invoice follows the same milestone.
