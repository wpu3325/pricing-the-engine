Pricing model

Each customer line is priced from its own cost, so the price follows the cost model. Human hours are never sold: setup, cycles and handoff are packages priced from standard effort, and automation shares its saving with the customer.

INTERNAL. Shows cost, margin and the GPU economics. Do not send this page to the customer.

1. From cost to price

Read top to bottom. Every line is its cost divided by (1 − its margin). Margins differ by line type: packages 45%, managed compute 30% (the most contested part of the price), platform and Ari usage 60% (the differentiated part).

7. Defensibility

  • Price band. $210,545 to $283,946 around $247,247 when throughput, GPU cost and effort each move 20% together. Quote the band until throughput is measured.
  • Floor. No line can be priced below its cost. The blended margin must stay above the 35% floor, or the quote is held as HOLD - MARGIN.
  • Competitor at 10% below. If a competitor prices at $222,522, margin falls to 38.5%. Match scope rather than rate: trading cycles or workflow scope keeps the economics intact.
  • Holds. The quote is held while the handoff has no holdout evaluation cost (HOLD - COST GAP), while token prices are unmeasured (HOLD - THROUGHPUT), or while the model is unverified.
