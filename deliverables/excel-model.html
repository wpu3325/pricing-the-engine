Internal. The workbook holds cost and margin. Do not send it to a customer.

How the workbook is laid out

  1. 01 Deal Setup. The inputs. Shaded cells are the only things to change: customer, model, cycles, throughput, costs, price, margin and the three approval gates.
  2. 02 Economics Capacity. All calculations. Rows 4–28 turn tokens into GPU-hours; rows 31–47 are the week-by-week capacity grid; rows 52–70 compare buying options; rows 73–85 are cost; rows 91–104 are price, margin and the status gate; rows 111–125 are optional production.
  3. 03 Offer and SOW. The customer-facing offer and SOW wording that the tool reproduces in its proposal and SOW documents.
  4. 04 Guide Assumptions. The guide and the assumption sources. The tool shows each input’s meaning and its workbook cell.
