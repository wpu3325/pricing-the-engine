PRELIMINARY: DO NOT SEND CUSTOMER: HOLD - MODEL

In short

You are buying 3 managed reinforcement-learning (RL) cycles to improve Held-out pass rate for Internal workflow automation, from a measured baseline of 60% toward a target of 80%, over 6 weeks. The model is Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, an open-weight model with 35 billion parameters, of which 3 billion are active for each token. The total is $247,247. The 80% target is a review point, not a guarantee: when it is reached, or when results stop improving, you decide whether to continue. You approve every cycle, and each invoice is issued when its milestone completes. The total below covers everything in the three phases; only usage above the included allowances and the items under Not included are extra.

Where your money goes

  • Setup package: evaluation and environment $49,449 (20%)
  • Managed RL cycles (3) $173,073 (70%)
  • Handoff and independent verification $24,725 (10%)

Words used in this proposal

RL cycle
One round of training on the model's own attempts, scoring them, and updating the model.
Rollout
One attempt by the model at a task, recorded with its result.
Held-out evaluation
A test set the model never trains on, used to measure real improvement.
Grader
The rules that score an attempt, for example pass or fail, or a rubric score.
Allowance
The compute and agent platform usage included in the price for the whole engagement. Usage above it is billed at the rates in the usage section.
Checkpoint
A saved version of the trained model, with its settings, so results can be reproduced.
Go / no-go gate
A decision point at the end of the foundation phase. You approve it before any cycle is paid for.
Change order
A short written amendment that adds or changes scope and its price before the work starts.
Statement of work (SOW)
The contract schedule that defines scope, phases, evidence, invoices and amendment rules.

Common questions

Why do I pay for the foundation before training starts?

Training is only as good as the test that measures it. The foundation builds that test, the grader and a baseline, and you keep them. It is the one part of the work that makes every later cycle measurable.

Is engineering billed separately?

No. Engineering is included in the setup and cycle prices. You are not invoiced for hours; each phase is priced for the work it delivers.

What is included in the total?

Everything in the three phases: setup, managed cycles with compute and platform usage up to the allowances, and handoff. Only usage above the allowances and the items under Not included are extra.

Why does the price depend on the model?

A larger model needs more compute for each cycle, so the managed cycle price is higher. The price shown already reflects the model you have chosen.

What if a cycle does not improve the score?

You decide after each cycle whether to continue, change direction or stop. You pay for the cycles and milestones completed. We do not stop contracted work silently, and we do not charge for work you have stopped before it starts.

What happens if I stop early?

You pay the invoices up to the stop point, and nothing more. Any third-party compute we committed for you on your approval is billed at cost. The acceptance payment is billed only on a signed handoff.

Acceptance

Each phase is accepted against the objective evidence listed in the statement of work, and each invoice follows the same milestone.
