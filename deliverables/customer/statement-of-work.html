3. Stopping rules and decision authority

  • Success target: held-out score of 80% on the frozen evaluation (Held-out pass rate). Reaching it triggers a review, not an automatic stop.
  • Plateau: gains below 1.0 percentage points for 2 consecutive cycles trigger a review.
  • Cycle cap: 3 cycles pre-authorised. No score threshold stops contracted work automatically.
  • Only the customer decides whether to continue or stop after each cycle. Decision owner: customer product / SME lead.
